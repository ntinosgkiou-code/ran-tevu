The insight

Booking AI is a commodity. The money is in what happens after.

Everyone automates the call that comes in. Almost no one touches the money that leaks out — the no-show nobody re-books, the patient overdue for a recall nobody phones, the after-hours call nobody returns.

The system

A closed loop — not a chatbot.

Ran-Tevu reads a clinic's own data, finds the leak, and proposes the fix. A human approves. The euro comes back. The dashboard keeps score.
