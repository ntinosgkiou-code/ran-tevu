Frequently asked questions

Is this HIPAA-compliant?

Our infrastructure is HIPAA-aware: EU-hosted, encrypted at rest and in transit, audit-logged, with optional BAA on the Multi-Location plan. We never store full conversation content longer than 30 days, and we never use patient data to train AI models.

Does it integrate with my practice management software?

Native: Google Calendar, Outlook, Calendly. Via Zapier/Make.com: Dentrix, Open Dental, Eaglesoft, Curve, Carestream. Custom integration available on Multi-Location plan.

What if the AI doesn't understand a caller?

If confidence drops below a threshold (e.g., heavy accent, complex insurance question), the AI says: "Let me transfer you to our team" and routes to your office or on-call number. No patient ever feels they're stuck with a bot.

How long does setup take?

1 business day. You give us: practice hours, services + duration, accepted insurance, calendar access, and any custom scripts. We provision a US phone number and route it. You can port your existing number later (free).

What if we already have a receptionist?

Most clients use Ran-Tevu for after-hours and overflow only. Your receptionist takes calls 9-5; Ran-Tevu picks up evenings, weekends, lunches, and when she's already on another line. Net effect: zero missed calls, same staff cost.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Month-to-month, cancel with one click. No long-term contract. First month is free regardless.
