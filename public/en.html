❓ FAQ

Everything you need to know

Does it really work in natural English?

Yes. We use Claude Sonnet 4.5 (Anthropic) for conversations and ElevenLabs for voice. Both are state-of-the-art for English. Try the live demo above — most prospects can't tell it's AI in the first 30 seconds.

What does "$3 per booking" mean exactly?

You pay $149 one-time setup. Then for each appointment our AI books into your calendar = $3. If we book 0 appointments → you pay $0. If we book 50 → $150/mo. Voice tier has $79/mo base (covers US phone number + ElevenLabs).

Is there a free trial?

No free trial. You pay $149 setup upfront. We activate within 24-48 hours. 14-day money back guarantee — full refund, no questions asked.

Do I need a website?

No. We give you a custom URL (yourname.ran-tevu.gr). Use it standalone, embed in your existing site (1 line of code), or just put the link in your IG bio.

How does it handle emergencies?

Fully customizable. AI recognizes keywords ("pain", "bleeding", "urgent") and either transfers the call to you immediately or escalates via SMS. You define the rules during setup.

How long is setup?

Chat Booking: 24 hours. Voice Receptionist: 48 hours (we provision the phone number). Digital Products: 24-72 hours. All remote — no on-site visits.

Where is my data stored?

EU servers (Ireland via Railway). GDPR-compliant. CCPA-compliant. Anthropic doesn't train on API data. Full data export available anytime, free.
