# Ran-Tevu Brand Guide

## Color palette

| Color | Hex | Usage |
|-------|-----|-------|
| **Primary Navy** | `#1a1a2e` | Backgrounds, primary text |
| **Royal Purple** | `#4a148c` | Accents, CTAs, highlights |
| **Signal Gold** | `#ffd700` | Logo accent, badges, highlights |
| **Cloud White** | `#ffffff` | Backgrounds, body text on dark |
| **Mist Grey** | `#f5f5f7` | Section backgrounds |
| **Slate** | `#6b6b80` | Secondary text |
| **Success Green** | `#16a34a` | Approve, positive metrics |
| **Alert Red** | `#dc2626` | Errors, urgency |

## Typography

- **Headings:** Inter (system-ui fallback), weights 700-800
- **Body:** Inter / -apple-system, weights 400-500
- **Greek headings:** Tone same, fallback "Roboto" works for Greek glyphs

## Logo files

- `/public/brand/logo.svg` — full lockup (mark + wordmark + tagline)
- `/public/brand/logo-mark.svg` — square icon only (for IG profile, favicon, app icons)

## Voice

**For Ran-Tevu (business account):**
- Confident, helpful, data-driven
- Specific numbers > generic claims
- "We help X businesses do Y" not "We are revolutionary"
- Greek + English fluent

**For Konstantinos (founder voice — emails):**
- Warm-professional
- First-person ("I built this because...")
- Personal observations from Crete/Greece SMB market
- Self-deprecating where appropriate

## Post visual templates (Canva specs)

- Title: 60px Inter Bold, color #1a1a2e
- Body: 32px Inter Regular, color #6b6b80
- Brand stripe top: gold (#ffd700) 8px height

### Instagram square (1080×1080)
- Solid navy background
- Title text: white, 80px center
- Brand mark bottom-right
- Optional gold accent line under title

### Facebook (1200×628)
- Same as LinkedIn but landscape
- Logo bottom-left

## Don'ts

- No founder face in social posts (per founder preference)
- No emoji bombs (3+ in a row)
- No "Στον σημερινό κόσμο..." Greek clichés
- No "In today's digital age..." English clichés
- No hype words: revolutionary, game-changing, cutting-edge
